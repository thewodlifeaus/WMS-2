TWLKITS
A kit is a SKU you sell (eg a 3-pack or a bundle) that has no stock of its own. When an order comes in with a kit SKU, the WMS swaps it for its parts, so pickers pick the parts and the pack desk scans the parts. Each line can have substitutes: if the main SKU is short, the first substitute with enough stock is used. Kits from SkuVault were imported; kits you create or change here are marked WMS.
